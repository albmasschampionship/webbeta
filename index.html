What is Global Manual Therapy

A platform, not just a competition

Global Manual Therapy is an international platform dedicated to elevating the standards of manual therapy worldwide. Through championships, congresses and educational initiatives, we connect professionals, celebrate excellence and promote innovation in health and wellness.

The Albanian Massage Championship is its flagship stage: a live, judged arena where technique, presence and client care are scored in front of a real audience — not a classroom.

  • Certified jury from six national federations, scoring on a published rubric.
  • Two competition days, open masterclasses and a Grand Final gala.
  • Every competitor leaves with a scorecard and written jury feedback.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Where exactly is the championship held?
The Palace of Congresses (Pallati i Kongreseve) in Tirana, Bulevardi Dëshmorët e Kombit. It is a 15-minute walk from Skanderbeg Square and 25 minutes by car from Tirana International Airport (TIA).
What is included in a spectator pass?
Access to the competition halls for the days covered by your pass, live scoring screens, and the open masterclasses as long as seats remain. Weekend and VIP passes also include the Grand Final and awards ceremony on 23 May.
How do I register as a competitor?
Choose “Competitor Entry” on the booking page. You will be asked for your certification, insurance and professional details, plus the category you want to enter. Your registration is reviewed by the organising committee before it is confirmed, and payment is taken up front to hold your slot.
Which payment methods are accepted?
All major debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) are processed securely by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe's hosted payment page and never touch our servers.
Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?
Spectator passes are refundable up to 30 days before the event and transferable to another person at any time — just email us with the booking reference. Competitor entries are refundable up to 45 days before the event; after that they can be transferred to another category subject to availability.
Is the venue accessible?
Yes. The Palace of Congresses has step-free access, accessible toilets and reserved seating. Write to us after booking and we will reserve the right seats and arrange assistance on arrival.
Do you provide accommodation?
We partner with hotels within walking distance and publish a booking code to ticket holders. VIP and competitor passes include a discount code for the official hotel.
